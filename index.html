Hoofbill

Fifty profiles. Twenty-five horses, twenty-five toucans, none of whom asked to be here.

  • Swipe right or tap ♥ to add someone to your Menagerie.
  • Swipe left or tap ✕ to pass — nothing is lost.
  • ↺ undoes the last swipe. Every profile stays in the Field Guide forever.
  • Tap any card to read the whole profile.
  • Arrow keys work too: ← pass, → smitten, ↑ read, U undo.

Every photograph is a real bird or horse, from Wikimedia Commons. The names, ages and opinions are entirely invented.
